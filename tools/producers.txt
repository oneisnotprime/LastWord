# The letter economy table: what every word that makes letters makes, and the words that hold
# land, lay roads and take cities.
#
# What every word that makes letters makes, by PLAN.md "Letter economy". Hand-written.
#
# build_demo.py first takes every produce_* value off every word (demo_words.txt and
# generated_words.txt alike; area operations such as DROUGHT's produce_A*0 are kept), then sets
# what this file says. A word not listed here makes no letters by itself. Each line is
#   word: property tokens        (the same tokens as demo_words.txt; they win over the word's own)
#
# Only the letters PLAN.md gives to things you own may be produced: A crops, I livestock and pets,
# P planted flora, W mills, M machines, C crafting stations, B furniture, N hired labour, Y age,
# and O (wells, lakes), G (quarries, mines) and U (caves, fungus) besides the hand actions.
# L, R, S, T and Z come from land, roads, cities, trades and bosses, never from a word's value.
#
# production_needs says what it takes (shared produce.ts reads these the same for every word):
#   tilled  stands on tilled land (tile_type=tilled tills its own)    water    yield follows moisture
#   labor   someone of its owner's side who works stands within reach  wind     yield follows the wind
#   flow    beside running water                                        claim    inside its owner's claimed ground
#   harvest pays once a cycle, to its owner's side standing within reach
#   extract runs down: halves every EXTRACT_HALF letters it gives
#   vowel / a letter: one used up from its owner for each letter made
# When a line gives no production_needs, the letter's own default applies (build_demo.py NEEDS).
# Living producers (livestock, workers) also need feeding, and nothing makes letters near a fight.
