# Theme 11, materials and elements (shared material.ts). A material noun before another noun, or
# an adjective whose material= names one, remakes the thing out of it: density moves mass, blows
# and speed; toughness (integrity) moves integrity; hardness moves armour and edges; brittleness,
# flammability, conductivity and a low melting point decide what hurts it (GLASS SWORD shatters,
# PAPER CASTLE burns, IRON DOG is armoured, slow and carries lightning). Its colour, value and
# own damage, resistances and statuses come with it (an ICE sword freezes, a SILVER one hurts the
# unholy). Materials are what transmuting to Z starts from.
